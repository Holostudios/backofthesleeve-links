Black Friday 2026 Vinyl Deals: Dates and Tips

Black Friday 2026 falls on Friday, November 27, and so does Record Store Day Black Friday, when independent shops sell limited vinyl releases in person only. Cyber Monday is November 30, and Bandcamp Friday, when Bandcamp waives its revenue share so artists keep more, falls on November 6 and December 4. This page is refreshed every week with what we can verify.

The dates that matter

  • Friday, November 6: Bandcamp Friday. Bandcamp waives its revenue share from midnight to midnight Pacific Time, though transaction fees still apply. The next one is December 4.
  • Thursday, November 26: US Thanksgiving.
  • Friday, November 27: Black Friday and Record Store Day Black Friday. The records are sold in participating independent shops, first come first served.
  • Saturday, November 28: Some shops put leftover Record Store Day stock online. Rough Trade says its online sales start at 8 a.m. GMT; the US listing we checked says 8 a.m. Eastern.
  • Monday, November 30: Cyber Monday.

The official lists were published on 6 October 2026. Releases are pressed in limited numbers for independent shops, there are no pre-orders, and quantities differ from shop to shop. A few rock titles on the official lists:

Check the official list for your country before you go, since the US and UK and Ireland lists differ. Find participating shops through the Record Store Day site.

How to tell a real vinyl deal from a fake one

  1. Compare against the price history, not the crossed-out number. A list price on a page is not proof the item was ever sold at that price.
  2. Check the format and pressing. A cheap listing may be a different edition, a CD, or an item that only shares the album name.
  3. Watch the seller line. Marketplace sellers can list the same album at wildly different prices.
  4. Decide your ceiling first. If you would not pay it on November 25, a sale label should not change your mind.

Quick answers

When is Black Friday 2026?

Friday, November 27, 2026, the day after US Thanksgiving. Cyber Monday is November 30.

When is Record Store Day Black Friday 2026?

Friday, November 27, 2026, in participating independent record shops. Some stock may appear online the next day.

Can I pre-order Record Store Day releases?

No. Record Store Day Black Friday releases are sold in person on the day in participating shops, first come first served.

When are the Bandcamp Fridays in 2026?

February 6, March 6, May 1, August 7, September 4, October 2, November 6 and December 4, each from midnight to midnight Pacific Time.
