Best Rock Band Biographies and Music Books: 9 Picks

Start with Please Kill Me (1996) for punk, Our Band Could Be Your Life (2001) for the American indie underground, Hammer of the Gods (1985) for Led Zeppelin, and It Starts with One (2024) for Linkin Park. The memoirs by Keith Richards, Bob Dylan, Patti Smith and Bruce Springsteen give the first-person view.

Oral histories and scene histories

These books tell a scene’s story through many voices rather than one author’s opinion.

Please Kill Me (Legs McNeil and Gillian McCain)

Please Kill Me: The Uncensored Oral History of Punk by Legs McNeil and Gillian McCain, first published by Grove Press in 1996. McNeil was one of the three founders of Punk magazine.

See it on Amazon

Our Band Could Be Your Life (Michael Azerrad)

Michael Azerrad’s Our Band Could Be Your Life: Scenes from the American Indie Underground, 1981–1991 (Little, Brown, 2001) profiles 13 bands, among them Black Flag and the Minutemen, and draws on interviews and old fanzines.

See it on Amazon

Meet Me in the Bathroom (Lizzy Goodman)

Lizzy Goodman’s Meet Me in the Bathroom (2017) is a history of the New York rock scene. It was adapted into a 2022 documentary of the same name.

See it on Amazon

Band biographies

Biographies show a band from outside. Read them with the author’s sources in mind.

Hammer of the Gods (Stephen Davis)

Stephen Davis’s Hammer of the Gods (1985) is an unauthorized biography of Led Zeppelin. Davis travelled with the band for two weeks at the start of its 1975 U.S. tour as a Rolling Stone journalist.

See it on Amazon

It Starts with One (Jason Lipshutz)

It Starts with One: The Legend and Legacy of Linkin Park by Billboard’s Jason Lipshutz was published by Hachette Books on October 1, 2024. For our own account of the band’s early near-misses, read The Bravest No.

See it on Amazon

Memoirs

Memoirs give the first-person version, so they are best read as one side of the story.

Life (Keith Richards)

Keith Richards’s Life, written with journalist James Fox, was published in October 2010. It runs from his childhood influences and discovery of blues music through the founding of the Rolling Stones.

See it on Amazon

Chronicles: Volume One (Bob Dylan)

Bob Dylan’s Chronicles: Volume One was published by Simon & Schuster on October 5, 2004.

See it on Amazon

Just Kids (Patti Smith)

Patti Smith’s Just Kids, published on January 19, 2010, documents her relationship with the artist Robert Mapplethorpe.

See it on Amazon

Born to Run (Bruce Springsteen's memoir)

Bruce Springsteen’s autobiography Born to Run was released on September 27, 2016, as a companion to his album Chapter and Verse.

Quick answers

Which rock book should I read first?

If you want one scene told by many people, start with Please Kill Me. If you want one band, pick the biography of an act you already love, then compare it with the band’s own memoirs.

Are unauthorized biographies reliable?

They can be, but treat them like any single source: check dates and claims against other books and interviews. Hammer of the Gods, for example, is described as unauthorized.

Is there a book about Linkin Park?

Yes: It Starts with One by Jason Lipshutz, published by Hachette Books on October 1, 2024.
