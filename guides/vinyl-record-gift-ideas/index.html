A good vinyl gift is a landmark album with a story, such as The Beatles’ Abbey Road (1969), Queen’s A Night at the Opera (1975) or Bruce Springsteen’s Born to Run (1975), paired with something that protects the record, like anti-static inner sleeves or a record washer.

Seven records with a story

We chose albums where the release details are well documented, so the gift comes with something true to say. Each link goes to a listing that passed our checks for artist, album and format. See below for how to check any other listing yourself.

Abbey Road (The Beatles) on vinyl

Abbey Road is the Beatles’ eleventh studio album, released on 26 September 1969 by Apple Records. It is the last album the group recorded, and it topped the charts in both the United States and the United Kingdom.

A Night at the Opera (Queen) on vinyl

Queen’s fourth studio album came out on 28 November 1975 on EMI in the UK and Elektra in the US. It was produced by Roy Thomas Baker and Queen, and it was reportedly the most expensive album ever recorded at the time of its release.

See it on Amazon

Born to Run (Bruce Springsteen) on vinyl

Bruce Springsteen’s third studio album was released on 25 August 1975 by Columbia Records. He co-produced it with his manager Mike Appel and the producer Jon Landau, after the commercial failure of his first two albums.

See it on Amazon

Sticky Fingers (The Rolling Stones) on vinyl

The Rolling Stones’ ninth studio album was released on 23 April 1971, the first on the band’s own new label, Rolling Stones Records.

See it on Amazon

Hotel California (Eagles) on vinyl

The Eagles’ fifth studio album was released on 8 December 1976 on Asylum Records. It was the first with guitarist Joe Walsh and the last with founding bassist Randy Meisner, and its cover is a photograph of the Beverly Hills Hotel.

See it on Amazon

The Rise and Fall of Ziggy Stardust (David Bowie) on vinyl

David Bowie’s fifth studio album came out in the UK on 16 June 1972 on RCA Records. Bowie and Ken Scott co-produced it, and it features his backing band the Spiders from Mars: Mick Ronson, Trevor Bolder and Mick Woodmansey.

See it on Amazon

Master of Puppets (Metallica) on vinyl

Metallica’s third studio album was released on 3 March 1986 by Elektra Records and recorded in Copenhagen with producer Flemming Rasmussen. It was the last album with bassist Cliff Burton, who died on 27 September 1986.

How to check a vinyl listing before you buy

  1. Artist and title match exactly. Search results mix in posters, shirts and unofficial items with the same words in the title.
  2. The format says vinyl or LP. Many albums are also sold on CD and cassette under nearly identical names.
  3. Single album or bundle? A double album or box set is a different, larger gift than one LP.
  4. It is sold and shipped by a seller you recognise. Look at the seller line near the buy button.
  5. You know the return window. Check it at checkout, especially for a gift.

Quick answers

What is a good first record to give someone?

A landmark album with a clear story is a safe choice: Abbey Road, A Night at the Opera and Born to Run are all documented in detail. Avoid greatest-hits sets if you do not know what they already own.

Are reissues worse than original pressings?

That depends on the pressing, and we do not rank pressings by sound. For a gift, the simplest choice is a current release that the listing clearly describes as new.

Do records need special care?

Yes. Inner sleeves protect against dust, static and scratches, and a record washer removes dirt before you play a record. That is why the guide pairs records with both.
