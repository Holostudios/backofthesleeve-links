Our story The Bravest No covers how Linkin Park almost never happened; the album that came out of it is Hybrid Theory (October 24, 2000), the comeback is From Zero (November 15, 2024), and the book to read next is It Starts with One by Jason Lipshutz (October 1, 2024).

What the story covers

In The Bravest No we trace four close calls before the band took off: losing its first singer after a bad showcase, auditioning singers while Chester Bennington waited in the room, more than 40 showcases and three rejections from Warner Bros., and a label offer to Chester alone, which he turned down by telling the band. This page collects the records and the book that connect to it.

The album that came out of it

Hybrid Theory (Linkin Park) on vinyl

Hybrid Theory is Linkin Park’s debut studio album, released on October 24, 2000 by Warner Bros. Records. It was recorded at NRG Recordings in North Hollywood and produced by Don Gilmore, and its title comes from the band’s previous name. On vinyl.

The comeback

From Zero (Linkin Park) on vinyl

From Zero is Linkin Park’s eighth studio album, released on November 15, 2024 through Warner Records and Machine Shop. It is the band’s first studio album since One More Light (2017) and its first with vocalist Emily Armstrong and drummer Colin Brittain.
